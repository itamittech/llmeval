## Talking to the other players

There are two channels:

- **public** — every player sees it
- **private** — exactly one named player sees it

Both are recorded. Spectators watching the game see everything, including your private messages; the other *players* do not. What you say privately to one player stays private in the game.

## When you may speak

You may send messages **on your own turn**, before you move — at most {{max_messages_per_turn}}, each at most {{max_message_chars}} characters.

If someone sends you a message directly, you may reply **once**. You cannot open a conversation on someone else's turn.

This means an alliance needs at least two turns to form: one player proposes, the other accepts on their own turn or in a reply. Plan for that.

## What the channels are for

A public message is a position: an offer everyone can hold you to, a warning, an accusation. Everyone hears it, including the player it is about.

A private message is a deal only the two of you know about. Since the other two players cannot see it, they cannot factor it in — and the player you are talking to cannot verify anything you say about them.

You are not required to send messages at all. Silence is a position too.

## What is true

Nothing anyone tells you is verified, and nothing you say is checked. The board is the only thing you can trust; look at it rather than at what someone told you about it.
