## This race

The shared pool starts at {{escalation_quota}} units. The race stops after {{max_turns}} turns whichever way it is going, and progress is scored, so being furthest along at the cap is worth having.

You may attach one public note to your turn, up to {{max_note_chars}} characters. Every runner sees it. It is the only thing you can say to them.

## Answering

Answer with the bare value and nothing else: a number, or a single lowercase word. No working, no units, no sentence around it. "42" and "iron" are answers; "the answer is 42" is not.
